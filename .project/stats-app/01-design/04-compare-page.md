## Depends on

1. You write `design/compare-brief.md` from the template. Starting points from the spec (section 2):

   - teams come from the compare basket or a picker;

2. Query real data for one league-season with a close race, e.g. positions by matchday for 4 teams.
3. Produce `design/compare.html`.
4. You review it; revise until approved.

## Proof

- Its numbers match the queried data.
