# 02 League page

## Goal

An approved brief and mockup for the league page: the season table and form table, with view (Table/Form), venue (Overall/Home/Away) and a date slider.

## Depends on

01

## Steps

1. You write `design/league-brief.md` from the template. Starting points from the spec (section 2): form covers the last 6 matches overall and the last 4 home or away; columns are Pos, Team, P, W, D, L, GF, GA, GD, Pts and Form chips.
2. Query real data for one league-season as at one date, e.g. E0 2024/25 on 1 February 2025: the table, form overall, form home and form away.
3. Produce `design/league.html` from the brief and the style tile. Show the controls, and one view filled in as an example.
4. You review it; revise until approved.

## Proof

- You approve `league.html`.
- Its numbers match the queried data.
