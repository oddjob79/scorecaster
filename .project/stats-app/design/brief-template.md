## Sites whose look you like, or describe the feel you want (optional input)
- https://myfootballfacts.com
    Likes:
    - Clear navigation
    - Looks good
    - Nice features - games
    - Nice presentation of interesting stats

    Dislikes:
    - Hero images too big (hero images at all)
    - Sorting teams alphabetically
    - Premier league focused

- https://footystats.org/
    Likes:
    - Quick navigation
    - Straight to useful information
    - Menu system confusing (two horizontal menus)
    - Very informative - information delivered with little fuss (statistical)

    Dislikes:
    - Finding more in-depth info more difficult
    - Many features locked by paid subscription

- https://www.statshub.com/
    Likes:
    - Looks great
    - Nice features (Trends, Props)
    - Non-subscription site (per league may office similar content to what I can offer at first)
    - Information clearly delivered and it looks good

    Dislikes:
    - Logos for leagues - not clear
    - Popups
    - Social media hookup
    - Navigation not the easiest


All in all, I liked the statshub.com site most. It combined good information coverage with a nice look and feel. The footystats probably had the best coverage, but they hadn't paid much attention to the look and feel of the site, and it felt much more functional as a result. In terms of it's content though, it was very thorough

Other sites to look at
soccerstats.com
fbref.com
